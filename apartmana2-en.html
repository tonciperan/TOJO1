Location

We are located at Bašelovići 12A, in the peaceful bay of Grebaštica surrounded by Mediterranean vegetation. In front of the house there is a beautiful landscaped garden. Grebaštica is a small tourist destination 14 km south of Šibenik, known for its secluded beaches and crystal clear sea.
